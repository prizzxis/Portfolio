Nova

Year
2024
Role
Creative Developer
Stack
GSAP · WebGL · Three.js
Link
Live Site ↗

Nova is an interactive WebGL experience — a world you move through, built for the launch of a design studio.

The idea was to make a portfolio launch feel like an event. I built a particle-driven scene in Three.js — thousands of points responding to the cursor — and choreographed the entire site with GSAP so text, camera and particles move as one composition.

Creative work lives or dies on performance, so the scene runs at a locked 60 fps on desktop and gracefully degrades to a static, still-beautiful fallback on low-powered mobile devices. No frame drops, no excuses.

Fig. 01 — Opening scene

The details mattered more than the spectacle: scroll velocity shapes the particle field, hover states hint at interactivity, and every transition respects reduced-motion preferences. It should feel like a film you can touch — not a demo reel.

Fig. 02 — WebGL scene

This project is where I fell in love with the intersection of code and craft — and it's the reason motion and interaction are now at the center of everything I build.
