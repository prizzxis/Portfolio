About

Creative developer with a design eye — I turn ideas into fast, accessible web experiences and obsess over the details.

I fell into the web a couple of years ago and haven't looked back. Since then I've shipped marketing sites, SaaS dashboards and interactive experiences for startups, studios and indie founders — and I'm slowly building my own component library so I can work faster and cleaner. I care about the invisible 90%: semantics, performance budgets, animation timing, and the thousand small decisions that make software feel considered.

Right now I'm deep in motion — GSAP timelines, scroll-driven storytelling, WebGL experiments — and getting more fluent in React and TypeScript by the week. When I'm not shipping, I'm breaking things on purpose and writing about what I learn.
